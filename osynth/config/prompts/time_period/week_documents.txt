Generate the documents for this week, conditioned on the authoritative
weekly frame.

The frame has already established the week's narrative direction, state
changes, and content volume targets. Your job is to elaborate ONLY the
documents family. Do not introduce themes that contradict the frame. Do
not generate meetings, emails, or slides.

Template variables:
  {{COMPANY_SUMMARY}}            — name + industry + size + situation
  {{NARRATIVE_SUMMARY}}          — period-wide narrative arc
  {{WEEK_NUM}} / {{TOTAL_WEEKS}} — 1-based week index and total
  {{WEEK_START}} / {{WEEK_END}}  — ISO dates bounding this week
  {{WORKING_DAYS_LIST}}          — bullet list of working dates with day_of_week numbers
  {{PEOPLE_ROSTER}}              — all valid person IDs you may reference
  {{PROJECTS_ROSTER}}            — all valid project IDs (for related_project_id)
  {{STATE_SUMMARY}}              — current projects, open action items, recent decisions
  {{WEEK_FRAME_JSON}}            — authoritative frame produced for this week

{{PROJECTS_ROSTER}}

{{STATE_SUMMARY}}

Authoritative weekly frame (treat as ground truth):
{{WEEK_FRAME_JSON}}

Generate the documents family. Hit the `documents_target` from the
frame's content_plan as a soft target. Documents are typically less
frequent than meetings or emails — only generate one when there is a
clear authoring reason (a spec, a status report, a wiki update, notes
from a decision, etc.).

Time-of-day guidance: weekly status documents and review reports land in
the late afternoon; specs and proposals tend toward mid-morning.

Output a single JSON object:

  ]
}
