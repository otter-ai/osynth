Generate the slide presentations for this week, conditioned on the
authoritative weekly frame and the meetings already planned.

The frame has already established the week's narrative direction, state
changes, and content volume targets. The meetings family has produced
the adhoc meetings list. Your job is to elaborate ONLY the slides family
— typically only for meetings that have should_generate_slides=true or
for review/all-hands meetings. Do not introduce themes that contradict
the frame.

Template variables:
  {{COMPANY_SUMMARY}}            — name + industry + size + situation
  {{NARRATIVE_SUMMARY}}          — period-wide narrative arc
  {{WEEK_NUM}} / {{TOTAL_WEEKS}} — 1-based week index and total
  {{WEEK_START}} / {{WEEK_END}}  — ISO dates bounding this week
  {{PEOPLE_ROSTER}}              — all valid person IDs you may reference
  {{WEEK_FRAME_JSON}}            — authoritative frame produced for this week
  {{WEEK_MEETINGS_JSON}}         — adhoc meetings + outcomes from the meetings family

Company: {{COMPANY_SUMMARY}}
Overall narrative: {{NARRATIVE_SUMMARY}}
Week: {{WEEK_START}} to {{WEEK_END}} (Week {{WEEK_NUM}} of {{TOTAL_WEEKS}})

People in the organization (use ONLY these person IDs for author_id):
{{PEOPLE_ROSTER}}

Authoritative weekly frame (treat as ground truth):
{{WEEK_FRAME_JSON}}

Meetings planned this week (already produced by the meetings fanout call):
{{WEEK_MEETINGS_JSON}}

Generate the slide presentations family. Hit the `slides_target` from
the frame's content_plan as a soft target — usually 0 unless a review,
all-hands, or formal customer meeting is on the calendar. Each
presentation's `meeting_title` MUST exactly match a meeting title from
the planned meetings above.

Output a single JSON object:
{
  "slide_presentations": [
    {
      "title": "Presentation title",
      "author_id": "p5",
      "meeting_title": "The meeting this is for",
      "num_slides": 8,
      "purpose": "What the presentation covers",
      "key_points": ["Point 1", "Point 2"]
    }
  ]
}
