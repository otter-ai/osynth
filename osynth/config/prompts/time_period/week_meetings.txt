Generate the adhoc meetings and meeting outcomes for this week, conditioned
on the authoritative weekly frame.

The frame has already established the week's narrative direction, state
changes, and content volume targets. Your job is to elaborate ONLY the
meetings family — adhoc meetings + outcomes for both adhoc and recurring
meetings. Do not introduce themes or events that contradict the frame.
Do not generate emails, documents, or slides.

Template variables:
  {{COMPANY_SUMMARY}}            — name + industry + size + situation
  {{NARRATIVE_SUMMARY}}          — period-wide narrative arc
  {{WEEK_NUM}} / {{TOTAL_WEEKS}} — 1-based week index and total
  {{WEEK_START}} / {{WEEK_END}}  — ISO dates bounding this week
  {{WORKING_DAYS_LIST}}          — bullet list of working dates with day_of_week numbers
  {{PEOPLE_ROSTER}}              — all valid person IDs you may reference
  {{PROJECTS_ROSTER}}            — all valid project IDs (for related_project_id)
  {{STATE_SUMMARY}}              — current projects, open action items, recent decisions
  {{EVENTS_THIS_WEEK}}           — narrative events scheduled this week
  {{RECURRING_MEETINGS_SUMMARY}} — recurring meetings that occur this week
  {{WEEK_FRAME_JSON}}            — authoritative frame produced for this week

Company: {{COMPANY_SUMMARY}}

{{PROJECTS_ROSTER}}

{{STATE_SUMMARY}}

{{EVENTS_THIS_WEEK}}

Recurring meetings this week:
{{RECURRING_MEETINGS_SUMMARY}}

Authoritative weekly frame (treat as ground truth):
{{WEEK_FRAME_JSON}}

Generate the meetings family. Hit the `meetings_target` from the frame's
content_plan as a soft target — vary by ±1 if the narrative demands.
Model a small amount of realistic disruption when appropriate: missed
meetings, skipped/canceled meetings, and join-late behavior. Outcomes
should cover both adhoc and recurring meetings.

Time-of-day guidance: pick `preferred_time` (HH:MM, 24h) based on
context. Urgent or incident calls early; reviews later in the day; vary
across roles so the day's timeline looks human.

For adhoc meetings, use descriptive free-form meeting_type labels and include structured labels:
- affiliation: "intra_team", "cross_team", "cross_department", "org_external", or "ambiguous"
- participant_count: "3+", "2", "single_speaker", or "primary_with_audience"
- purpose: "training", "routine_monitoring", "problem_solving", or "information_giving"
