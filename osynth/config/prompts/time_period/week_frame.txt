Generate the authoritative frame for this week of the workspace simulation.

This frame establishes shared scope for parallel family-specific generation
calls (meetings, emails, documents, slides) that follow. The frame is the
source of truth on the week's narrative direction, state changes, and
volume targets — fanout calls must defer to it rather than independently
deciding the week's global scope.

Template variables:
  {{COMPANY_SUMMARY}}            — name + industry + size + situation
  {{NARRATIVE_SUMMARY}}          — period-wide narrative arc
  {{WEEK_NUM}} / {{TOTAL_WEEKS}} — 1-based week index and total
  {{WEEK_START}} / {{WEEK_END}}  — ISO dates bounding this week
  {{WORKING_DAYS_LIST}}          — bullet list of working dates
  {{STATE_SUMMARY}}              — current projects, open action items, recent decisions
  {{EVENTS_THIS_WEEK}}           — narrative events scheduled this week
  {{RECURRING_MEETINGS_SUMMARY}} — recurring meetings that occur this week
  {{PREV_FORWARD_BRIEF}}         — running narrative context curated by last week's planner

Output a single JSON object capturing only the week-level frame. Do NOT
list individual meetings, emails, documents, or slides — those will be
generated by family-specific calls that condition on this frame.

The `forward_brief` field is the running narrative context that next
week's planner needs to stay coherent — distill what is still in motion,
what has just resolved, and what should not be forgotten.

The `content_plan` block expresses per-family volume targets and shared
priorities. Pick targets that are realistic for the company size,
narrative momentum, and number of working days. A typical week has a
small handful of adhoc meetings, a few email threads, and occasionally a
new document. Slides are rare — usually only for review or all-hands
meetings.
