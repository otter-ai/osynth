`specialized_terms` guidance:
- 6-10 entries: vocabulary a team member would actually say aloud in a standup, design review, or status update for this project.
- Mix two flavors. Aim for roughly half-and-half:
  1. `industry_term` — real domain terminology a practitioner in this field would recognize but an outsider would not: technical concepts, protocols, regulatory acronyms, compound nouns, named methodologies, vendor / tool names that are genuinely used in the field.
  2. `internal_coinage` — internal-to-this-company vocabulary: codename-style project nicknames, internal component or service names, in-house acronyms, version handles (e.g., a renamed module, an internal tier name, a numbered initiative).
- `expansion` is required for acronyms and codename-style nicknames (so the canonical phrase is preserved); set it to null for plain compound nouns or single-word terms that are not abbreviations.
- Pick terms that pattern-match how that domain actually talks. Hardware teams talk about chip steppings and bringup; legal teams talk about statute codes and motions; biotech teams talk about assays and dosing regimens. Avoid generic business words like "roadmap" or "milestone".
- Do NOT just paraphrase the project description; these should be additional vocabulary the team uses while discussing the project, not restatements of what the project is.
