Generate ONLY the company-wide recurring meetings — the rituals that involve leadership or the whole company:
- All-hands / company-wide meetings
- Executive / leadership team weekly sync
- Department head reviews
- Any other meeting that spans the entire company or its leadership

Do NOT generate per-team rituals (1x1s, standups, team syncs) or cross-functional / external meetings — those are handled separately.

The meeting_type field is free-form — use descriptive labels like "all_hands", "exec_sync", "leadership_review", "department_review", etc.

For each meeting, also provide structured labels:
- affiliation: "intra_team", "cross_team", "cross_department", "org_external", or "ambiguous"
- participant_count: "3+", "2", "single_speaker", or "primary_with_audience"
- purpose: "training", "routine_monitoring", "problem_solving", or "information_giving"
- topic: free-form topic category
- roles: list of participant roles in this meeting context

Constraints:
- No person should have overlapping recurring meetings at the same time
- All meeting times must be within working hours and end before working hours end
- Use realistic durations: all-hands 45-60min, exec syncs 30-60min

Output a single JSON object:
{
  "recurring_meetings": [
    {
      "id": "rm_company_1",
      "title": "All Hands",
      "meeting_type": "all_hands",
      "organizer_id": "p1",
      "attendee_ids": ["p1", "p2", "p3"],
      "cadence": "weekly",
      "day_of_week": 4,
      "time_of_day": "15:00",
      "duration_minutes": 60,
      "description": "Weekly all-company meeting",
      "labels": {
        "affiliation": "cross_department",
        "participant_count": "primary_with_audience",
        "purpose": "information_giving",
        "topic": "Company Updates",
        "roles": ["executive", "employee"]
      }
    }
  ]
}

Valid cadence values: "daily", "weekly", "biweekly", "monthly"
For "daily" cadence, day_of_week should be null.
For "weekly"/"biweekly", day_of_week is 0=Monday through 4=Friday.
For "monthly", day_of_week represents the preferred weekday.
